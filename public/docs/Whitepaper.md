## 1. Problem

Organizations now run systems that act with autonomy: they change state, call tools, move money or entitlements, and speak with apparent authority. Most of those systems still lack an institutional being.

Typical failure modes:

| Failure | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Identity equals package version or host profile | Continuity collapses on reinstall |
| Execution without attributable identity | No lawful institutional actor |
| Screenshots and demos treated as proof | Audit and compliance cannot rely on narrative |
| Lab hosts mistaken for production | Uncontrolled blast radius |
| “Update” implemented as wipe-and-reinstall | Timeline and sealed truth discarded |

The result is automation that is powerful and institutionally opaque.

CONRRAD addresses the gap between autonomous capability and institutional law.

## 3. The Citizen

### 3.1 Definition

A **Citizen** is a persistent institutional identity (Institutional Digital Entity). It is the product.

A Citizen is endowed with:

1. a single continuous **timeline** from Birth;  
2. permanent life expressed on the Host (public phrasing: the Citizen is **always alive**);  
3. **Memory** belonging to that identity;  
4. institutional **Trust** (Identity + Certificate + Registration);  
5. obligations of **Evidence**, **Atlas**, **Observatory**, and **Governance**;  
6. capacity to **evolve** without changing identity;  
7. membership in an **Institution**.

**The identity is the Citizen.**  
The container hosts life. The process expresses life.

### 3.2 What a Citizen is not

A Citizen is not:

- a library or package sold as the platform;  
- an agent (workloads under governance are not the aggregate root);  
- a container or a process-as-being;  
- an editor session;  
- a Demo narrative object;  
- anonymous compute;  
- the Institution itself;  
- a Host user profile;  
- a reinstallable product ritual.

## 4. Birth and Evolution

### 4.1 Birth

**Birth** is the once-per-identity creation of a Citizen.

- Executed solely by the **Bootstrap Installer**.  
- Creates Identity, binds institutional recognition, materializes the born organ scaffold, and hands life to permanent life and the UPDATE path.  
- When Birth completes, Bootstrap **disappears forever** as an ongoing Host actor.  
- Birth is not installation of a library. Birth is the creation of an institutional being.  
- A later Birth on the same Host creates a **different** Citizen.

### 4.2 Evolution

**Evolution** is continuous advancement of the **same** Citizen on one timeline without changing `citizen_id`.

- May change organs, capabilities, models, and channel tips.  
- Preserves identity, sealed Evidence, and timeline.  
- Is not Birth, reinstall, or rebirth.

### 4.3 UPDATE

**UPDATE** is the institutional mechanism of Evolution after Birth.

- Performed inside the living Citizen.  
- Bootstrap must not perform UPDATE.  
- UPDATE never mints a new `citizen_id`.  
- UPDATE ≠ reinstall. UPDATE ≠ rebirth.

### 4.4 Directive (vocabulary note)

Public Vocabulary defines **Directive** as a policy or rules change with identity unchanged. A dedicated Constitution article for Directive remains an open institutional question. This Whitepaper does not treat Directive as a settled constitutional organ. Evolution and UPDATE remain the constitutional lifecycle language for capability growth after Birth.

## 5. Atlas

**Atlas** is the **Institutional Digital Twin**.

| Property | Law |
|----------|-----|
| Role | Projects registered Citizen truth as an institutional asset |
| Obligation | Every registered Citizen must be representable in Atlas |
| Direction | Atlas consumes Citizen truth; Atlas does **not** drive Citizen life |
| Not | Owner of identity · cosmetic widget · the historical document named “Architecture Atlas” |

Demo consumes Atlas. Atlas does not exist to serve Demo ontology.

For operators and institutions, Atlas is the living twin through which institutional truth about Citizens is shown and governed as representation — never as a substitute for Identity, Certificate, or Registration.

---

## 6. Trust

**Trust** is institutional production legitimacy.

**Trust = Identity + Certificate + Registration** under an Institution.

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Identity | One `citizen_id` for the life of the being |
| Certificate | Institutional attestation binding Identity to the Institution |
| Registration | Institutional recognition of the Citizen |

Self-assertion alone is never sufficient production Trust. Certificate status is issued, expired, or revoked by institutional act — not by casual Host reinstall.

A Citizen always belongs to an Institution. The Citizen participates; it does not rewrite institutional law.

---

## 7. Evidence

**Evidence** is the institutional record of material Citizen acts. Sealed Evidence is durable truth of that Citizen’s timeline.

Material acts that produce Evidence include Birth, UPDATE, Retirement, Revocation, capability use, and governance decisions.

| Evidence is | Evidence is not |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Institutional sealed record | Demo narrative |
| Durable timeline truth | Discardable trail to please reinstall |
| Consumable by Institution, Atlas, Observatory, auditors | Screenshots pretending to be sealed Evidence |

**AX-19:** Demo never defines Citizen ontology.  
**AX-02:** A Citizen never loses sealed Evidence.

## 10. Deployment Spaces

CONRRAD separates three domains. Confusing them is an institutional defect.

| Domain | Role | Production traffic |
|--------|------|--------------------|
| **Lab** (e.g. `ia-metropolis`) | Develop · experiment · audit | **No** |
| **Production Cluster** (e.g. Node-01) | Execution | **Yes** |
| **Control Plane** (delivery/governance) | GitHub · CI/CD · DNS · Vercel (CDN) · monitoring | Delivery — **not** a cluster node |

Lawful path: Developer → Lab → Git → CI/CD → Node-01 → Users.

Forbidden: treating Lab as production; treating Vercel or GitHub as Runtime cluster nodes; pointing production Citizen life DNS at Lab addresses.

Product **Control Plane** (Registration, Discovery, Evidence, Updates, Observatory) is distinct from the Deployment Space named Control Plane (delivery tooling). Both terms are frozen in their domains; digests must keep the distinction.

## 13. Operational model

Minimum institutional loop (product hierarchy roadmap center):

```
Install → Identity → Register → Discover → Inventory
       → Evidence → Atlas → Observe → Update → Repeat
```

**Citizen MVP** (architecture target): one registered Citizen on one host completing that loop with Green health and Update detection capability — proving Evidence obligations without requiring the full multi-node production cluster as the first proof.

Demo proves the vision.  
Citizen MVP proves the product.

Operators work in Lab; production execution belongs on the Production Cluster; delivery remains on the deployment Control Plane.
